# Sources

Frameworks agreed on 17-10-2026. They were picked for how directly they apply to work email, not for bestseller rank, which could not be verified for any of them. Every link below was checked on 17-10-2026 and returned 200.

| Layer | Framework | Book | First-party link |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gate | Six principles: less is more, make reading easy, design for easy navigation, use enough formatting but no more, tell readers why they should care, make responding easy | *Writing for Busy Readers*, Todd Rogers and Jessica Lasky-Fink (2023), based on their behavioural research | https://www.hks.harvard.edu/publications/writing-busy-readers |
| Structure | Pyramid Principle, SCQA, MECE | *The Pyramid Principle*, Barbara Minto | https://www.barbaraminto.com |
| Structure | BLUF, bottom line up front | US Army correspondence rules (AR 25-50). The official text could not be opened from here, so the skill uses BLUF as a rule and quotes nothing from it. | n/a |
| Format | Tease, lede, why it matters, go deeper | *Smart Brevity*, Jim VandeHei, Mike Allen, Roy Schwartz (Axios) | https://www.axioshq.com/smart-brevity-book |
| Craft | Subject lines, plain words, tone, trimming | *HBR Guide to Better Business Writing*, Bryan A. Garner | https://store.hbr.org/product/hbr-guide-to-better-business-writing/10946 |
| Module: asks | Seven principles of persuasion | *Influence*, Robert Cialdini | https://www.influenceatwork.com/7-principles-of-persuasion/ |
| Module: follow-ups | No-oriented questions, labels, accusation audit, calibrated questions | *Never Split the Difference*, Chris Voss | https://www.blackswanltd.com |
| Module: difficult | Start with heart, STATE, make it safe, contrasting | *Crucial Conversations*, Patterson, Grenny, McMillan, Switzler | https://cruciallearning.com/crucial-conversations-book/ |

The "Have you given up on this project?" line is widely attributed to Voss, but no first-party source for it was found. The skill presents it as an example of his technique, not as a quote.

Left out on purpose: AIDA and PAS (marketing copy, cold outreach is covered by `executive-communication`), Strunk and White and Zinsser (general writing, already covered by Rogers and Garner), Nonviolent Communication (mostly overlaps Crucial Conversations).
