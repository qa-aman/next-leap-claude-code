Head-coupled perspective

Holographic Window

Your webcam tracks your head. The renderer rebuilds an off-axis projection frustum every frame, so the screen behaves like a window onto a 3D Gaussian splat instead of a flat picture. Video never leaves the browser.

Needs localhost or HTTPS for camera access. First load pulls the MediaPipe model (~4 MB).
