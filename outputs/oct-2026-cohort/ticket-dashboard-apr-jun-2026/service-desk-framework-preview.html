1. Bottom line

BLUF · Minto

Two of last quarter's three calls moved the right way: urgent (P1) tickets now meet SLA, and Password tickets are falling. Laptop got worse, and a June spike in VPN tickets is the new risk.

Three decisions for this quarter

  1. VPN: Network Operations to check the VPN client version rolled out from May. 10 tickets name it, and VPN tickets tripled in June.
  2. Laptop: Desktop Support to bring a recovery plan. Only 12 of 31 Laptop tickets met SLA, down from 13 of 26.
  3. Automation: pause the Password automation build. Volume already fell from 35 to 15 a month, and the bigger agent hours sit in Laptop, ERP and Licensing.

What was supposed to happen

Fix P1 SLA, reduce Laptop and Licensing breaches, and take Password tickets off the desk.

What happened

P1 recovered and Password fell. Licensing improved a little. Laptop slipped further, and VPN tripled in June.

Why it was different

The export doesn't say what changed. These need confirming with each service owner: did a Password self-service flow go live in May, what changed for P1, and why Laptop breaches rose.

What to do next quarter

Keep what fixed P1. Move the focus from Password to Laptop and VPN. Agree targets now (section 6), so the next review has numbers to check against.

Rule for the skill: each quarter opens with the previous report's calls and a result for each. A "why" from the data is labelled as a hypothesis until the owner confirms it.

4 · Business resultNot in the export

Needs cost per agent hour or user downtime, which the export doesn't carry.

The fix worked, but Password was never where the hours are. Each Password ticket takes about 0.8 hours. This quarter Laptop used 707 agent hours, ERP System 494, Licensing 483 and VPN 360, against 55 for Password.

Rule for the skill: every past "Decision" gets this 4-step chain the following quarter, so a fix is judged on hours freed, not only on volume.
